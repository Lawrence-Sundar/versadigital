What This Is

A Structured Partnership, Not a Free Trial

We are onboarding a small number of founding clinic partners — independent practices that want to be early adopters of intake automation in exchange for a reduced commercial arrangement. This is not a generic free trial. It is a structured 60-day partnership with clear terms on both sides. We cover the build. You give us the opportunity to demonstrate results.

02
What You Get

What Is Included

  • -Full intake automation system built and configured for your clinic
  • -Setup completed within 5–7 working days of onboarding

05
After 60 Days

What Happens at the End

At the end of the 60 day period we will have a short review call. If the system has delivered value and you want to continue, you move to standard monthly monitoring at £150/month. If it has not delivered what we promised, you walk away. No invoice, no obligation. We keep the case study either way. That is all we ask.

06
Data & Privacy

GDPR Compliance

All patient enquiry data processed through this system is handled in compliance with UK GDPR. No patient data is stored beyond what is required for enquiry logging. Data is processed through UK-based infrastructure and is never shared with third parties. Full compliance documentation available on request.
